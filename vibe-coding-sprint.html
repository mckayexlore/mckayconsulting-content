5 Days. One Wicked Problem. Real Code.

In the AI Crucible, the traditional model of Plan-then-Build is a structural risk. Most organisations are amnesiac — they plan for months but fail to learn from what they ship.

The Vibe Coding Sprint flips this by applying the PAK Model: Practice → Application → Knowledge. We don’t teach you to be innovative; we steady the bicycle while you find your balance on a real, high-stakes problem.

The Five Days

Each day applies one of the Five Mindsets to a live challenge inside your organisation. The work is cumulative — each day builds the conditions for the next.

Day 1 Radical Empathy & Worldview Witnessing
Mindset Move from “what customers say” to “what customers do”.
Activity The Product Trio — Product, Design, Engineering — enters the user’s environment for in-field interviews and radical witnessing.
Goal To understand the customer’s worldview, values, and unspoken constraints — not just their feature requests.
The Shift Moving beyond proxied understanding to a direct claim on the truth of the customer’s daily reality.
Day 2 Vibe Ideation & Intuitive Hacking
Mindset Generative divergence over evaluated convergence.
Activity Using Vibe Coding — leveraging AI as an augmented brush — teams rapidly translate Day 1 insights into functional concepts.
Goal To explore a meaningful solution space by building multiple, divergent ideas that address the prioritised problem landscape.
The Shift Replacing rigid specifications with high-velocity, intuitive “pokes” to the system to see what resonates.
Day 3 Strategic Filtering & The “No” Discipline
Mindset Rigorous discipline in saying no to maintain focus.
Activity We evaluate the Day 2 vibe-concepts against the organisation’s Strategic Learning Map and desired impacts.
Goal To kill ideas that dilute focus and select the single direction most likely to heal a Wicked Problem.
The Shift Shifting from a responsive culture that tries to please everyone to a criteria-driven culture of impact.
Day 4 Coded Iteration & Adoption Pressure Tests
Mindset Treating early versions as learning instruments, not compromises.
Activity Parallel work-streams. One track iterates on the product prototypes in real code. The second conducts intense adoption tests — inside the organisation’s backend systems and at the client organisation.
Goal To ship a Minimum Learnable Product that is technically viable and operationally ready.
The Shift Moving from polish-first to evidence-driven iteration in real-time.
Day 5 Solution Demo & The Strategic Alignment Meme
Mindset Storytelling as a strategic capability.
Activity A formal demo of the coded solutions followed by accelerated storytelling. We use AI to synthesise the week’s learnings into a Strategic Meme — a narrative and visual map for rollout.
Goal To secure buy-in through a coherent story that explains what we believed, what we tried, and how we will anchor this new practice.
The Shift Ending organisational amnesia by turning a five-day experience into an aligned, permanent cultural reflex.
