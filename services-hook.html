How I Work

The work takes different forms depending on what is needed. Some engagements are focused and advisory in scope. Others are longer, involving an organisation's leadership across time. In either case, the work begins from actual conditions, not frameworks applied from the outside.

Executive Programme

Some engagements call for something different in kind.

For senior leaders facing transformation pressure that touches identity as much as strategy — where the familiar tools no longer reach the actual problem — this is an offer of a different order. A private, residential programme for a small group of leaders at equivalent seniority. Not strategy consulting. Not leadership training. A structured space in which a more fundamental quality of thinking becomes possible, at the moment when that quality is exactly what the situation demands.

Michael McKay has been in these rooms, with these leaders, at these moments. This programme draws directly on that experience.
